About me

I'm Xinran Bi. I work on AI safety and alignment at Shouxu Tech.

My work focuses on building and evaluating guardrails, detecting behavioral risks, and developing defenses for large language models (LLMs) and AI agents. My applied research investigates attack surfaces and explores how to translate legal norms, ethical principles, and broader human values into practical safeguards.

I received my Master of Engineering in Computer Science from Princeton University and my B.A. in Mathematical Statistics and Computer Science from Smith College.

My earlier research spans software engineering and computational biomedicine. At Smith, I worked with Alicia M. Grubb's lab and the Veterinary and Biological Informatics Lab. I also worked with the Jian Carrot-Zhang Lab at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center during the summer of 2023 and after graduation.
